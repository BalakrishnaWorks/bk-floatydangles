
# ✦ BK FLOATYDANGLES

### A little charm. A little motion. A lot more personality.

A lightweight Windows desktop application that brings playful floating charms and smooth, hanging motion to your everyday screen.

**Developed by Balakrishna**

---

## ✦ Welcome to BK FLOATYDANGLES

Your desktop deserves a little personality.

BK FLOATYDANGLES lets you choose from a collection of charm designs, interact with your favourite, and enjoy its natural swinging movement right on your desktop.

Simple to use, playful by design, and made to add a little character to your screen.

## ✨ Features

### 🎨 Choose Your Charm
Explore the available charm designs and switch between them whenever you want. Find the one that feels right for your desktop.

### 🪢 Interactive Movement
Enjoy smooth, hanging and swinging movement that brings your chosen charm to life. Drag and position your charm on the screen.

### ❌ Closing Confirmation
A confirmation option helps you avoid closing the application accidentally.

### 🖥️ A Personal Desktop Experience
Keep your favourite charm on your desktop and enjoy a small touch of creativity while using your computer.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Download the official Windows installer from the **Releases** section of this repository.
2. Open the downloaded installer.
3. Follow the installation instructions.
4. Launch BK FLOATYDANGLES.
5. Choose your favourite charm and start interacting with it.

**Simple. Playful. Yours.**
